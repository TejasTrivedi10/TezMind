Glassmorphism UI kit

Frosted, translucent mobile web components floating over color: background blur, light borders and one violet gradient accent. Outfit for headings, Inter for text. Everything is interactive and follows light and dark mode.

Glass recipe

Fill
white 9% dark, 42% light

18px, saturate 160%
Border
1px white 20% to 75%
Radius
14 fields, 24 cards, 28 sheets
Shadow
0 8px 28px + top highlight

Keep text on glass at 4.5:1 or better. Raise the fill opacity when the background behind is busy.
